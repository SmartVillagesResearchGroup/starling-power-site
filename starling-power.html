About

A Tanzanian Company
Built on Proven Expertise

Starling Power is a newly established Tanzanian renewable energy company, created to deliver high-quality commercial solar solutions for organisations with demanding power requirements.

The company has spun out of more than a decade of applied research, system design, and field implementation led by Smart Villages, in close partnership with Tanzanian organisation OMASI. This work has been funded by the UK Government and international partners, and has delivered robust solar and hybrid energy systems across Sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia.

Starling Power has been formed to translate this proven technical expertise into a dedicated, locally registered commercial entity — capable of contracting, installing, operating, and maintaining large-scale solar systems in Tanzania.

Projects

Early Projects &
Growth Pipeline

Starling Power enters the market with active momentum. As of Q1 2026, the company has seven commercial solar installations in development — designed to offset large diesel gensets — with several additional projects under advanced negotiation.

All systems are being designed to meet international standards, prioritising:

  • Power continuity and redundancy
  • Long-term operational reliability
  • Reduced fuel costs and emissions
  • Practical operation and maintenance in remote environments
