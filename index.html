About Starling Power

A Tanzanian Solar Company

Starling Power Ltd is a Tanzanian company that was created out of renewable energy industrial research and development projects in Sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia.

We deliver high-quality commercial solar for organisations with demanding power requirements. We are a locally registered company, able to contract, install, operate and maintain large-scale solar systems anywhere in Tanzania.
