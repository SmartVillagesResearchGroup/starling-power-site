Our innovation

Engineering Experience Across Africa and Asia

Our team has a breadth of engineering expertise, with 9 years' experience designing and installing custom made solar systems in Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, South Sudan, Somaliland, Nigeria, Eswatini, Lesotho and Malaysia.

Our solar systems have powered rural off-grid community radio stations, dairy processing plants, health facilities, boreholes, milling machines, sub-villages and refugee camps.

Our systems range from 10 kWp to 150 kWp, with over 1.5 MW installed by Q2 2026. Innovations among these installations include:

  • Floating solar
  • Non-permanent structures
  • Remote monitoring and support
  • Hybrid systems
  • Quality battery expertise
  • Backup inverter systems
  • Battery management systems
  • Battery banks
  • Circularity

Current Projects and Growth Pipeline

As of Q3 2026, Starling Power has seven commercial solar installations in development, designed to offset large diesel generators, with several more projects under advanced negotiation.

Every system is designed to meet international standards, prioritising:

  • Reduced fuel costs and emissions
  • Practical operation and maintenance in remote environments
