How it works

Build, Own, Operate, Transfer

Through our Build, Own, Operate, Transfer (BOOT) model, communities pay nothing upfront.

1. Build

We install the solar system and rehabilitate the borehole where needed.

2. Own

Starling Power finances and owns the system, so the community pays nothing upfront.

3. Operate

A water agent from the local community is trained to run the site, selling water on a pre-paid basis.

4. Transfer

Once costs are recovered, the assets are transferred to the community.

Value for money

Value in Every Cent Spent

  • $5 lifetime capital cost per person gaining improved water access
  • Capital cost recovery in under 2 years, ready for reinvestment into new water projects
  • 5% water sales commission paid directly to the local community water committee, enabling savings for future maintenance and training

Technology

Borehole Data Collection, Monitoring and Control

We have developed the in-house technical expertise to make a customisable, low-cost monitoring and control device. It ensures reliable, sustainable operations, without prohibitively high hardware and subscription costs.

Why monitor

  • Understand typical usage, informing future designs for maximum cost efficiency and usefulness
  • Remote troubleshooting, reducing technician travel costs
  • Early warning when components near the end of their life
  • Accurate energy data, with carbon credit and DREC potential

Data logger

  • Cross-compatible with inverters supporting Modbus data exchange, including Grundfos
  • Customisable logging frequency and data
  • Local offline logging to SD card
  • Real-time online visualisation via cellular data or wifi
  • Customisable visualisations for different applications

Remote control

  • Time-based shut-off for any inverter
  • Offline shut-off using unlock tokens entered locally
  • Remote shut-off where there is cellular or wifi connectivity
  • Shut-off when an energy or water counter level is reached, for inverters with Modbus data logging
